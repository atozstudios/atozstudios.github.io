Numova

Puzzle · 2048 Merge

Slide, merge, and think ahead. Combine matching tiles, chain your merges, and push for the 2048 tile — then keep going.

  • Four ways to play — Classic, Time Attack, Blockers and a Daily Challenge everyone shares.
  • Three board sizes — 4×4, 5×5 and 6×6, each with its own best score.
  • Three themes — Classic, Dark and Ocean.
  • Take back a move — step back through recent moves instead of restarting a good run.
  • Plays offline — no account, no sign-in. The leaderboard is opt-in and off by default.

Pinball Rush

Arcade · Classic Pinball

Classic arcade pinball with the ease of a casual game. Flippers, bumpers, and a space-adventure table built for one more round.

  • Classic pinball, hyper-casual — the arcade format without the learning curve.
  • A variety of tables — each with its own design and features.
  • Real pinball physics — with simple, intuitive controls.
  • Leaderboards — beat your high score, then climb against everyone else.
  • Retro presentation — neon visuals and arcade sound.

About

A small studio, two games, and room for more.

A2Z Studios is an independent developer publishing on Google Play. We work on one idea at a time and keep it small enough to finish properly — a puzzle you can play in a queue, an arcade game you can play with one thumb.

How we build

  • Playable in seconds. Nothing to set up before you play — no onboarding carousel, no tutorial standing between you and the game.
  • Yours to keep private. Numova plays entirely offline; its leaderboard is off unless you switch it on, and you can remove your scores at any time.
  • Finished, not endless. We would rather ship four solid game modes than twenty half-built ones.

Bug reports, ideas, or anything else — email atozstuduios@gmail.com. We read everything, even when we cannot reply to all of it.
