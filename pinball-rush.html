What's in it

  • Classic pinball, hyper-casual

    The arcade format without the learning curve. Pick it up, pull back, and play.

  • A variety of tables

    Each table has its own design and its own features to learn.

  • Real pinball physics

    Weight and bounce behave the way they should, with simple, intuitive controls.

  • Leaderboards

    Beat your own high score, then climb against players everywhere else.

  • Retro presentation

    Neon table art and arcade sound effects, in the spirit of the originals.

Built for one more round

Pinball Rush takes the excitement of a pinball machine and drops it into something you can play standing up, one-handed, for ninety seconds. There is nothing to unlock before you start and nothing to read before you understand it — the ball is already in play.

Whether you grew up in front of a real cabinet or have only ever seen one in a film, the game is aimed at both: forgiving enough for a first go, and deep enough that a good run feels earned.
